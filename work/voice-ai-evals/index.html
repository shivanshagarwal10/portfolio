CASE STUDY 06 — AI EVALUATION

Voice AI Evals

A hybrid framework for post-production collection calls that separates business outcome, borrower intent, conversation quality, technical performance and compliance—then traces every semantic failure back to transcript evidence.

OVERVIEW

A score is not enough. Teams need the failure story.

Voice AI collection calls can complete a task while still mishandling a borrower, exposing sensitive information or creating a poor spoken experience. This framework models those concerns separately instead of compressing them into one opaque success value.

Each call becomes an auditable evaluation with canonical outcomes, stage-level judgments, technical signals, normalized failure types and transcript evidence. Campaign aggregation then shows which failures happen most often and which ones are associated with unresolved calls.

ARCHITECTURE

Objective telemetry and semantic judgment, together.

Call + telemetry→Deterministic evaluator+Semantic evaluator+Compliance evaluator→Policy normalization→Campaign analytics

Latency, silence and connectivity stay deterministic. Outcomes, objection handling and harmful language use LLM judgment. Critical compliance findings apply hard score caps so task completion cannot hide unsafe behavior.

EVALUATION MODEL

Different questions get different evaluators.

BUSINESS

Outcome and intent

Separates borrower state from final resolution and distinguishes task resolution from collection success.

CONVERSATION

Stage-level quality

Opening, verification, discovery, objections, resolution, commitment, confirmation and closing are scored independently.

TECHNICAL

Voice telemetry

Deterministic rules handle latency, silence, interruptions, connectivity and metadata failures.

COMPLIANCE

Independent safety path

Privacy disclosure, coercion, sensitive information and misleading claims are evaluated separately.

EVIDENCE

Auditable failures

Semantic findings include turn IDs, customer text, agent text and a plain-language explanation.

CAMPAIGN

Root-cause analytics

Aggregations connect failure frequency and severity to resolution impact across a campaign.

DASHBOARD

From campaign health to one broken turn.

  • Connection, resolution, collection-success and compliance-failure rates.
  • Business outcome, payment intent and final resolution distributions.
  • Pass, partial, fail and not-reached status for every conversation stage.
  • Top failure modes ranked by frequency, severity and impact on resolution.
  • Critical-call review with transcript evidence and compliance violations.
  • Individual-call drill-down alongside a 10-call sample and 10,000-call synthetic demo.

VALIDATION

Evaluation quality is evaluated, too.

A manually labelled golden dataset checks outcome, payment intent, task resolution and expected key failures. The included sample reports 8/8 on all three classification checks, while tests cover evaluator behavior and scripts validate, aggregate and generate demo campaigns.

The repository clearly labels the 10,000-call campaign as synthetic demonstration data generated from evaluated call archetypes—not production traffic.
